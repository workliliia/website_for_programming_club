President of the Programming Club

Welcome to the Programming Club at the University of the West of Scotland (UWS)! Our president, Liliia Rudenko, is passionate about helping students develop their coding skills and explore new technologies. Under their leadership, we organise weekly meetups, coding challenges, and collaborative projects that create a supportive environment for all members—whether you’re a total beginner or an experienced programmer.

Join us to network with like-minded enthusiasts, work on open-source initiatives, and grow your technical skills. Together, we aim to build a strong community of students who love to learn, share knowledge, and innovate at UWS. Reach out to us if you’d like to get involved or have any questions!
